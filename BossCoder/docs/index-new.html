What is the eligibility criteria for the program? +

For Freshers Program: BE/BTech/MCA/BCA or any graduate with basic programming knowledge. For Working Professionals: Any IT professional with at least 6 months of experience.

Do you offer placement assistance? +

Yes! We provide 100% placement assistance with mock interviews, resume building, and direct referrals to our 400+ hiring partners.
